# Assignment 4b: Frontend UI Coding

## User Journey

Amy recently took a trip up the east coast to visit her sister at MIT, stopping along the way in NYC for the first time. She had a wonderful adventure but is worried that she'll soon forget the details of her travels.

She opens up the Away app on her laptop, which takes her to the Login page. She enters her username and password and is taken to her empty Postcards page. She clicks the "+" button, and fills out the title (Nights in NYC), location (New York, New York, United States), travel dates (10/1/25 to 10/2/25), and description (An amazing stop in NYC -- delicious foods and lots of sights!) for her visit to NYC. She clicks the "Create Post" button and is taken back to the Postcards page, where she can now see her first post displayed. She repeats the process to create another postcard for her stay in Boston, reminiscing about her favorite moments (not included in video because of time).

The user wants to return to Massachusetts soon to visit places like Concord and Salem, so she navigates to her Wishlist page to add both of the destinations (Concord, Massachusetts, United States and Salem, Massachusetts, United States).

Finally, she navigates to the Friends page to see what her sister May has posted recently. She sees May's recent trip to Rhode Island, as well as the same two Massachusetts towns on her wishlist (which they're planning to visit together). The user is satisfied that she was able to record the memories from her recent travels for herself and others to always look back on, and she's excited for future adventures ahead.
