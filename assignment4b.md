# Assignment 4b: Frontend UI Coding

## User Journey

Amy recently took a trip up the east coast to visit her sister at MIT, stopping along the way in NYC for the first time. She had a wonderful adventure but is worried that she'll soon forget the details of her travels.

Amy opens up the Away app on her laptop, which takes her to the Login page. She enters her username and password, which brings her to her currently empty Postcards page. Amy clicks the "+" button to create a new post, and fills out the title (Nights in NYC), location (New York, New York, United States), travel dates (10/1/25 to 10/2/25), and description (An amazing stop in NYC -- delicious foods and lots of sights!) for her visit to NYC. Then, she clicks the "Create Post" button and is brought back to the Postcards page, where she can now see her first post displayed. She repeats the process to create another postcard for her stay in Boston(not included in video because of time), reminiscing about her favorite moments!

Amy would love to return to Massachusetts soon to visit places like Concord and Salem, so she navigates to her Wishlist page. She uses the form fields to add both of the destinations (Concord, Massachusetts, United States and Salem, Massachusetts, United States) to her wishlist.

Finally, Amy navigates to the Friends page to see what her sister May has posted. She sees May's recent trip to Rhode Island, as well as the same two Massachusetts towns on May's wishlist (which they're planning to visit together). Ultimately, Amy is satisfied that she was able to record the memories from her recent travels for herself and others to always look back on, and she's excited for future adventures ahead!
