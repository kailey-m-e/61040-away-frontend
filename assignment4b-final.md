## User Journey

Laura, an MIT student in Camp Kesem, just traveled to the Kesem National Leadership Summit and got to visit Illinois for the first time! She had a wonderful adventure but is worried that she'll soon forget the details of this trip.

Laura opens up the Away app on her laptop, which takes her to the Login page. She enters her username and password, which brings her to her Postcards page. Laura clicks the "+" button to create a new postcard, and fills out the title (Kesem National Leadership Summit), location (Carlinville, Illinois, United States), travel dates (10/24/25 to 10/26/25), and description (Had a magical time and met so many new Kesem members!) for her trip. Then, she clicks the "Post" button and is brought back to the Postcards page, where she can now see her new postcard displayed.

Laura saw the famous St. Louis arch from the bus window on the way to the airport, but would love to return to visit for real. She also really wants to visit Chicago! After navigating to the Wishlist page, she uses the form fields to add both of the destinations (St. Louis, Missouri, United States and Chicago, Illinois, United States) to her wishlist.

Finally, Laura navigates to the Friends page to see what her friend Amy has posted. She took a trip to Amsterdam in the summer, and it seems like one of her bucket list destinations is Paris! Ultimately, Laura is satisfied that she was able to record the memories from her recent trip for herself and others to always look back on, and she's excited for future adventures ahead!
